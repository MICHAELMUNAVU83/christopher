## About Section (Carousel)

### Who are the beneficiaries of the Christopher Msando Foundation

The Foundation aims to provide exposure and skills to youth and children with little to no access to technology. These skills are to help increase their quality of life, grow their talents and passions and nurture them in character and purpose.

### Who is the CMF Foundation Investor

The CMF Foundation Investor is one interested in making an impact on young people in underserved communities. They are interested in promoting innovation, creativity and learning opportunities to those who have less access to it. They are interested in making a difference.

### Who was Christopher Msando

Christopher Msando was the Information Communication Technology (ICT) Manager at the Independent Electoral and Boundaries Commission (IEBC) at the time of his demise on 28th July 2017. He was a hardworking man who was keen on details, resilient, honest, a straight talker and forthright, decisive, an initiator, loving and empathetic. That is why he excelled in what he did.

### Why the Christopher Msando Foundation

The Christopher Msando Foundation was established in his honor and legacy, to advance causes he believed in, encourage the youth into ICT and better innovation platforms to bring a common bonding cause.

## Programs and Initiatives

### 1. Youth Mentorship Programs

We aim to grow not just skilled professionals in the technology sector but also young capable leaders full of character. We would like to create a community of people who would love to pour into young people and nurture them peace, conflict resolution, music, leadership and personal finance management.

_[Learn More]_

### 2. Volunteer's Day

The Foundation plans to create an environment where volunteers develop as well from giving to young unequally advantaged children and youth such that they are able to share life experiences and skills learnt from the work at the Foundation.

_[Learn More]_

### 3. ICT Training

With your support, we would love to develop an ICT Centre that trains youth and children who would otherwise have little access to technology in skills that would greatly improve their quality of life. Through grants and donations we would be able to identify needy beneficiaries and hone their potential to become the next change makers in the country.

_[Learn More]_

### 4. Cultural Exhibitions

To promote the awareness of the legacy of various Kenyans such as Christopher Msando, who have made contributions to the development of the country in various sectors among the youth, we would love to organise spaces where young people can tell African Stories, review Kenyan History and as a result, breed inspiration and belief in their ability to do great things.

## Visual/Design Notes (for reference when rebuilding)

- **Color palette:** White background, blue/navy gradient accents (light blue to deep blue), black text, black-and-white treated photography for some program sections
- **Typography:** Clean sans-serif for body/nav; a handwritten/marker-style font for section headlines (e.g., "Transformational Technology", "Youth Mentorship Programs", "Contact Us")
- **Imagery:** Large portrait of Christopher Msando (halftone/high-contrast treatment) on homepage hero; photos of children/students in school uniforms; photos of Kenyan towns (Nairobi skyline, Siaya lakeside/street scenes) for the Flagship Cities section
- **Recurring motif:** A flowing blue ribbon/wave graphic used as a section divider/background element
- **UI patterns:** Carousel sections with left/right arrow navigation; card-based FAQ grid with icons; rocket icon used for Mission/Vision blocks; Impact Meter uses flip-counter style number displays
